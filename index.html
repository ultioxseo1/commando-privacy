Privacy Policy

Commando Run Online · Developer: Azra · Effective date: October 22, 2026

In short: Commando Run Online does not ask for your real name, email address, location, contacts or photos. We use an anonymous player ID, the nickname you choose, and your race times to run the online features. The game shows ads (unless you buy PRO), and our ad partner Google AdMob collects device data to serve them.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the mobile game Commando Run Online (the "Game"), published by Azra ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at yalcin.catar@aebdonusum.com.

4. Advertising

The free version of the Game shows interstitial ads between missions. Buying Commando PRO removes all ads. On iOS we do not request permission to track you across other companies' apps (App Tracking Transparency), so ads are shown without using your device's advertising identifier for cross-app tracking. You can also limit ad personalization in your device settings (iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking / Apple Advertising; Android: Settings › Google › Ads). Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), ads may be shown as non-personalized until consent is given.

5. Public information

Your nickname and race times are visible to other players on the leaderboard and in multiplayer. Please do not use your real name or any personal information as your nickname. Offensive nicknames may be removed.

6. Data retention and deletion

Gameplay and leaderboard data are kept while you play the Game. Settings stored on your device are deleted when you uninstall the Game. To request deletion of your online data (player ID, nickname, scores and cloud save), email us at yalcin.catar@aebdonusum.com with the nickname you use in the game. We will delete it within 30 days.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to certain processing. Contact us at the email above to exercise these rights.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored by our service providers using industry-standard security measures. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this address, with the effective date shown at the top.
